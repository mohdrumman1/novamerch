Custom hoodies

Custom hoodies work as club, staff and fundraising merchandise. The right fabric weight and print method decide how they look and last.

Get a free three-product mockup pack

Best for

  • Sports clubs and school groups
  • Staff and gym teams
  • Fundraising and supporter runs

How to choose

Fabric weight
Heavier fleece is warmer and feels premium. Lighter hoodies suit milder climates and layering.
Fit and sizing
Order a spread of sizes. For clubs, collect sizes first and include kids sizes if needed.
Print or embroidery
Large artwork suits print. Small chest logos suit embroidery.
Placement
Chest, back, sleeve and hood are common. More placements add cost.

Decoration methods

  • Screen printing for bold, repeat runs
  • DTF or digital transfer for multi-colour artwork
  • Embroidery for small chest logos

Not sure which suits your logo? Read embroidery vs screen printing vs DTF.

What to send us

  • Your logo (vector .ai, .eps or .pdf is best; a clear PNG works to start)
  • Rough quantity and budget
  • Your deadline and delivery suburb
  • Size breakdown if you have it

Price, minimums and production timing depend on the product, decoration method, quantity and supplier availability. We confirm these in your quote before you commit. Colours are matched as closely as the material and process allow, and we confirm the result with you before production.

Questions

Which is better, print or embroidery?

Print suits large and colourful artwork. Embroidery suits small logos and a premium feel. See our guide to printing methods.

Can we order mixed sizes?

Yes, hoodies are normally ordered with a size breakdown.

What is the minimum order?

It varies by garment and method. Tell us your quantity and we will confirm.
