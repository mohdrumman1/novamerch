Promotional pens

Promotional pens are a low-cost, practical item for events, reception desks and customer handouts.

Get a free three-product mockup pack

Best for

  • Events and trade shows
  • Reception and client handouts
  • Large giveaways

How to choose

Style
Plastic pens suit large giveaways. Metal pens feel more premium for clients.
Ink
Check ink colour and writing feel, not only looks.
Print area
Pens have a small print area, so keep the logo simple and legible.
Quantity
Pens are normally ordered in larger runs.

Decoration methods

  • Pad or screen printing for logos
  • Laser engraving on metal pens

Not sure which suits your logo? Read embroidery vs screen printing vs DTF.

What to send us

  • Your logo (vector .ai, .eps or .pdf is best; a clear PNG works to start)
  • Rough quantity and budget
  • Your deadline and delivery suburb

Price, minimums and production timing depend on the product, decoration method, quantity and supplier availability. We confirm these in your quote before you commit. Colours are matched as closely as the material and process allow, and we confirm the result with you before production.

Questions

Can you print a full-colour logo on a pen?

Some pens suit it. Tell us your logo and we will recommend an option.

What is the minimum order?

It varies by pen. Tell us your quantity.

Can we have different pens per team?

Often, yes. Ask and we will confirm.
