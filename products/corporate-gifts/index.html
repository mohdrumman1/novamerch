Corporate gifts

Corporate gifts work best when they are useful, suit the recipient and are presented well. A curated pack often beats a single item.

Get a free three-product mockup pack

Best for

  • Staff onboarding and recognition
  • Client thank-you gifts
  • Events and end-of-year gifting

How to choose

Recipient
Match the item to who receives it: new hire, client or sponsor.
Budget per pack
Set a per-person budget first, then build the pack to fit.
Presentation
Packaging and a simple card change how a gift feels.
Timing
Gifting seasons are busy. Share your date early so we can say if it is realistic.

Decoration methods

  • Mixed decoration across items (engraving, print, embroidery)
  • Branded packaging or inserts where available

Not sure which suits your logo? Read embroidery vs screen printing vs DTF.

What to send us

  • Your logo (vector .ai, .eps or .pdf is best; a clear PNG works to start)
  • Rough quantity and budget
  • Your deadline and delivery suburb
  • Who the gift is for and the occasion

Price, minimums and production timing depend on the product, decoration method, quantity and supplier availability. We confirm these in your quote before you commit. Colours are matched as closely as the material and process allow, and we confirm the result with you before production.

Questions

Can you build a mixed pack?

Yes. Mixed packs are a common brief. Tell us the budget and recipients.

Can each pack be personalised?

Sometimes. Tell us what you want and we will confirm what is possible.

What is the minimum order?

It varies by item and pack. Tell us your quantity.
