# Phase A warm/community experiment — 10 October 2026

## Objective

Test whether a permission-first, value-led offer creates qualified three-product mockup requests without cold email, paid spend or automated DMs.

## Offer

NovaMerch will prepare a free three-product mockup pack and indicative pricing for a club, team or local business. The recipient supplies a logo, rough quantity and deadline; nothing is produced without approval.

## Tracking URL

`https://novamerchau.com/free-mockup/?utm_source=warm-community&utm_campaign=phase-a-value-first-2026-10&utm_medium=organic&utm_content=three-product-mockup-v1`

## Approved post/reply copy

> Planning new uniforms, event merch or a sponsor pack? NovaMerch can put together three practical branded product ideas with indicative pricing, so you can compare options before committing. Request the free mockup pack here: [tracked link]. No obligation.

Reply template for an interested person:

> Thanks — send through your logo, the rough quantity, what the merch is for and when you need it. We’ll prepare three options and indicative pricing for review. We won’t contact you about anything unrelated to this request.

## Operating rules

- Publish only in a warm/community channel where NovaMerch is already allowed to participate; no scraping, unsolicited DMs or mass posting.
- Do not publish until the owner approves the exact channel and copy.
- Every response is handled by a human or reviewed draft on the same business day.
- Record source, campaign, copy version, consent/basis, response, quote value, direct cost, estimated gross profit, outcome and next action.
- Follow up at 48 hours and seven days only where the person requested the pack and has not opted out.
- Any opt-out, bounce or complaint suppresses further contact immediately.
