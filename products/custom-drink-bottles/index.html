Custom drink bottles

Branded drink bottles are one of the most-used items in a merchandise pack. They suit clubs, gyms, workplaces and events because people keep and reuse them.

Get a free three-product mockup pack

Best for

  • Sports clubs and junior registration packs
  • Gyms and member programs
  • Staff onboarding and events

How to choose

Material
Stainless steel keeps drinks cold or hot longer. Plastic and Tritan-style bottles are lighter and usually lower cost. Aluminium is light and takes bright colour.
Capacity
Common sizes are around 500 to 750 ml. Bigger suits training and sport, smaller suits desks and events.
Lid and spout
Sports spouts suit training. Screw caps suit desks and travel. Check what the club or team will actually use.
Colour
Bottle colour and print colour both matter. Dark bottles with light print are usually clearest.

Decoration methods

  • Screen or pad printing for simple logos
  • Laser engraving on metal bottles
  • Full-colour wrap or digital print for detailed artwork (availability depends on the bottle)

Not sure which suits your logo? Read embroidery vs screen printing vs DTF.

What to send us

  • Your logo (vector .ai, .eps or .pdf is best; a clear PNG works to start)
  • Rough quantity and budget
  • Your deadline and delivery suburb

Price, minimums and production timing depend on the product, decoration method, quantity and supplier availability. We confirm these in your quote before you commit. Colours are matched as closely as the material and process allow, and we confirm the result with you before production.

Questions

What is the minimum order?

It varies by bottle and decoration method. Tell us your quantity and we will confirm suitable options.

Can you match our club colours?

We work from your colour references and confirm the closest achievable result on the chosen bottle and method before production.

Are bottles dishwasher safe?

It depends on the bottle and the decoration. We will check this for the specific option you shortlist.
