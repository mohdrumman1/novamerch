# NovaMerch local SEO and AEO content plan

27 September 2026 · Newcastle and Maitland first

## What Jev is, and where it belongs

Jev is TypeSafe AI's System One decision model. It receives a state and focused typed questions, then returns a structured choice, score or probability. `jev-ultrafast` demonstrates this inside a browser agent: the page becomes an indexed list of legal controls, Jev chooses an operation and target, and a small language model is used only when text needs to be typed.

For NovaMerch, that means Jev is suitable for bounded review and routing, not for inventing marketing claims or sending messages. A future Jev pilot could score whether an enquiry is complete, route a request to clubs or businesses, flag a delivery-risk record for human review, or choose which draft needs attention. Code and human approval must still control permissions, prices, dates, suppression and publishing.

## Current SEO finding

The storefront has useful page content, metadata and a strong mock-up offer, but the live site was serving an older deployment on 27 September 2026. The live domain returned 404 for `/sitemap.xml` and `/robots.txt`. The repository now generates both routes, adds page-level metadata and exposes a small, fact-based JSON-LD graph for the organisation, website and FAQ. Redeploy and verify those URLs before expecting search engines to use the fixes.

## The free local growth loop

1. Make the Newcastle and Maitland offer clear on the home page and sports-club page.
2. Publish one genuinely useful page each week, not a suburb-keyword clone.
3. Link each page to the mock-up builder or quote form.
4. Ask every completed customer for a review and, with permission, a factual project note.
5. Share each useful page with relevant clubs, sponsors, chambers and community groups where it is genuinely helpful.
6. Use Search Console queries, enquiry source and quote outcomes to decide what to improve.

## First six content pieces

| Piece | Real question it answers | Evidence needed | Conversion path |
|---|---|---|---|
| Newcastle sports club merchandise ordering guide | How do we organise sizes, colours, sponsor logos and deadlines? | NovaMerch order brief, supplier-confirmed process, no invented lead times | Club mock-up request |
| Maitland business uniform starter guide | What should a small team order first? | Three real product pathways and budget caveats | Business quote request |
| Cooks Hill United FC project note | How was a 570-shirt order planned and delivered? | Written permission, verified quantity, approved images and timeline | Similar club enquiry |
| Custom merch checklist for events | What must be confirmed before ordering branded event items? | Artwork, quantity, audience, delivery location and supplier checks | Event-pack mock-up |
| Reorder system for clubs and growing teams | How do we make the next order easier? | Approved artwork/specification workflow actually used by NovaMerch | Reorder consultation |
| Bottles, caps, apparel or bags: choosing by use case | Which product makes sense for players, sponsors, staff or attendees? | Product catalogue facts, use-case comparison and limitations | Product shortlist |

Each article should open with a direct two-sentence answer, identify NovaMerch and the Newcastle/Maitland context, use descriptive headings, show the date reviewed, link to the relevant offer, and state any uncertainty. Do not claim “best”, “fastest”, “cheapest” or guaranteed delivery without evidence.

## AEO rules for every page

- Answer one clear buyer question near the relevant heading.
- Use real quantities, process details and examples only when verified.
- Name the organisation, service area and product relationship clearly.
- Keep FAQs visible and useful; structured data must match visible text.
- Add internal links from guides to the mock-up builder, sports-clubs page and quote path.
- Add author/reviewer and updated dates only when a real person has reviewed the page.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, duplicated suburb pages, fake reviews, hidden text and AI-written claims without evidence.

## Deployment and measurement checklist

- Deploy the storefront changes.
- Check `/sitemap.xml`, `/robots.txt`, canonical URLs and page titles in production.
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console.
- Verify the Google Business Profile name, category, service area, photos, hours and review replies.
- Track page, source, segment, enquiry, qualified opportunity, quote and order in Airtable.
- Review the same local query set monthly; do not treat one ranking snapshot as proof of a trend.
