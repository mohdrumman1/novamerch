## Required evidence before using this as the north-star order profile

- decoration method and all local fulfilment/payment costs;
- real buyer role and purchasing trigger;
- actual decision outcome, sales cycle and objections;
- reorder/referral signal; and
- written permission before using the customer name, logo, photos or result in marketing.

## Working replacement benchmark for the pilot

Target only local organisations with a documented trigger and potential for a repeatable group order. A lead qualifies for manual review only when all are true:

1. identifiable group-order use case (season, onboarding, event, sponsorship or uniform refresh);
2. plausible buyer/contact route;
3. contact basis is recorded and permitted;
4. no suppression, opt-out, hard bounce or recent unanswered sequence; and
5. the expected quote can support an acceptable gross-profit target after product, decoration, freight, payment and fulfilment costs.

The first five qualified quotes, rather than an unproven historical lead, become the evidence base for the A$10k/month model.
