## Preparation before a quote

- Check the current thread for any newer response before using this draft.
- Obtain the buyer's required band type, resistance levels, ball sizes, quantities, intended use, logo and date. Ask only for information not already supplied.
- Compare at least two suitable supplier options where available: material/specification, branding method, sample availability, minimum quantity, production timing and landed cost.
- Check product quality and suitability evidence for studio use. Do not claim medical suitability, certifications or safety properties without substantiation.
- Include the existing 10% offer in the margin calculation. Confirm what the discount applies to before producing the final quote.
- Quote actual product and freight costs, tax treatment and realistic timing; do not copy unverified historical catalogue prices.
