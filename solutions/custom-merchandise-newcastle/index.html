Newcastle merchandise questions

Who does NovaMerch supply custom merchandise to in Newcastle?

NovaMerch is based in Newcastle and works with local businesses, sports clubs, gyms, trades and event organisers. We have also supplied clubs outside Newcastle, because we deliver across Australia.

How do I get started?

Send your logo, your rough quantity, your budget and your deadline through the free mockup form. We review the brief and reply with three product ideas and rough pricing. There is no commitment.

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Minimums vary by product and decoration method. Tell us the quantity you have in mind and we will confirm suitable options before you commit.

Can you match our brand colours?

We work from your logo and colour references and confirm the closest achievable result on each product and decoration method before production. Exact matches depend on the material and process.

How long does custom merchandise take?

It depends on the product, the decoration method, the quantity and the artwork approval. Tell us your deadline in the form and we will say whether it is realistic before you order.

Do you deliver outside Newcastle?

Yes. NovaMerch is Newcastle-based and delivers across Australia.
